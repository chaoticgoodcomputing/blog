Privacy Policy

Spencer Elkington 2026-02-04

This site doesn’t collect your email address. However, if you sign up for the email service, I do keep the email on my own mailing list. I send out emails through Buttondown, which both protects your data while it’s in the mailing list and allows you unsubscribe at any time. If you do unsubscribe, I don’t (slash can’t) retain your email address.

As an optional part of this site, I have an email newsletter that you can subscribe to. If you do, your email is not handled by me. That is handled by Buttondown, an email subscription service. You can view their privacy statement at Buttondown: Privacy.
