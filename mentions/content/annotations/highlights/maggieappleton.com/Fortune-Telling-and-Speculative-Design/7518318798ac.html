“Not invented here” syndrome

I can’t believe this is the first I’m hearing of the formalized “not invented here”.

This has almost certainly gotten worse in the last 12 months, with the rise of decompilation/“clean room” re-ideation. I’ve seen it a lot on an interpersonal level, even: the whispers of “why would I adapt to this tool, when I could make a bespoke one myself?”

There is almost certainly a new “generate” option to the old buy versus build debate that has dialed up NIH instincts to 11.
