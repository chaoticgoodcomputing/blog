  • Herbert Simon — zesty! I know him primarily as one of the inventors of the linked list, a seminal data structure that every intro Computer Science student learns about. I had no idea that he was related to economics — although I shouldn’t be surprised. Part of the reason I chose economics as a field of study is because John von Neumann was responsible for many decisions in early computing architecture, and he also co-authored the seminal economics text Theory of Games and Economic Behavior with Oskar Morgenstern. The concept of expected utility came from that text — the full title is a von Neumann-Morgenstern utility curve
    1. My closest guess would be something related to a brief mention of land economics and redistribution of rent.
    2. I do want to note, however, that he also contributed to research in industrial organization — precisely the field of work covered by Evolution and Revolution as Organizations Grow.
