I recently got into a discussion about computer-aided software engineering with some folks, recently, for… reasons that should be obvious.

This bit of the paper feels prescient when you read it out of context, but it’s so, so important to realize that none of the recent discussion around AI in software engineering is new. Part of the background on Programming as Theory Building was the context in which the paper was written. In the mid 1980s, this was at the beginning of CASE and a lot of promises around the newest chip generation having certain capabilities around expert systems and rules-based programming. It didn’t pan out then, but here we are now.
