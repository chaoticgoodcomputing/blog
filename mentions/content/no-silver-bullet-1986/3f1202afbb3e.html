For some context: Brooks is the author of The Mythical Man-Month, originally published in 1975, about engineering and project management. It’s been awhile since reading it (“awhile” meaning 2022, at the recommendation of my manager at the time), but the book’s core premise is that adding more engineering hours to a project doesn’t necessarily make it complete faster: a combination of an irreducible core of work, with added management overhead in parallelization and coordination of work, may end up delaying it.
