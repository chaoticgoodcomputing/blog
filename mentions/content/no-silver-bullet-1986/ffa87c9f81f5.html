The techniques used for speech recognition seem to have little in common with those used for image recognition, and both are different from those used in expert systems.

Another thing that definitely didn’t age as well.

A really good book on how the history of this approach flipped from “expert-designed expert systems” to “throw as much data as humanly possible to train on” is The Worlds I See by Dr. Fei-Fei Li, who famously put together the ImageNet dataset. It was the performance of AlexNet on the ImageNet competition that kinda kicked off this whole deep-learning, neural-networking, transformers-are-all-you-need era of computing.
