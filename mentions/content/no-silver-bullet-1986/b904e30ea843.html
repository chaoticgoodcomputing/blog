This is also an important addition to the footnote earlier, where I qualify that speed ≠ quality. Quality of software, alone, is a horribly tough thing to try and quantify, and an additionally hard thing to factor into something like deployment lead time. It’s not un-benchmarkable: known benchmarks like the DX Core do an advanced set of development KPIs, but one of the key ones – time developing features, versus time developing fixes – is easy enough to do a napkin-level guesstimate if your company uses something like Conventional Commit standard or comprehensible semantic versioning. In those cases, it’s the ratio of feature: to fix:, chore:, perf:, etc.
