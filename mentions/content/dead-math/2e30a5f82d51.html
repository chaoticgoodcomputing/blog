Math is Dead, and Pickles are Small

Spencer Elkington 2026-10-26

Fuzzing is a very specific type of software test, where you bombard a piece of an application with a bunch of random inputs in the hope of crashing it. This visualization generalizes further than just fuzzing, though. Any type of declarative testing (where you are defining specific inputs and outputs, then checking for a pass or fail) can be visualized, mentally, with this model. You’re just testing specific branches of the application to see if it generally does what you’ve specified, but that’s not a complete description of the program.
