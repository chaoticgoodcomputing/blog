Math is Dead, and Pickles are Small

Spencer Elkington 2026-10-26

If we think about a post-Stokes world, I’d say that this is proof that the supply of “math”, in the abstract, has gone up thanks to AI agents. I don’t believe that this cheapens math: on the contrary, it may, in a Jevon’s Paradox situation, become more in demand. As focus shifts onto the specification of programs, with mathematical definitions of those specifications being in demand for the world’s most important and load-bearing programs, I can hardly see a future where “mathematician” becomes among the most important occupations a person could hold.
