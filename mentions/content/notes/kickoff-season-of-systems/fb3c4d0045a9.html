Kickoff: Season of Systems

Spencer Elkington 2026-01-04

  • As the boundary of what work can be handed off to these systems shifts, my (or anybody else’s) role in work also shifts. I feel that we’re hitting a plateau on the S-curve of capability for LLMs. I emphasize the “capability” part of that statement — I’m talking about the raw capability of how good an answer an LLM could provide when afforded merely the ability to raw-dog a prompt. I feel that the focus has shifted into tooling for additional context fetching and orchestration systems for multi- (or cross-)session systems.
