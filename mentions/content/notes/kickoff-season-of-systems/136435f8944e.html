Kickoff: Season of Systems

Spencer Elkington 2026-01-04

Generative AI has come quite a long way from simple chat interfaces. At that point, I think that folks (including myself) thought the primary shift would be what we, now, call AI slop. That’s still absolutely the case, but I find myself at the center of where AI, specifically AI agents seems to have found its most solid footing — software engineering and white-collar work. As these systems become more competent, it’s been a rapid rush to find what the human role in their use becomes.
