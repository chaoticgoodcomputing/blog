Quartz Widgets: Graphs, Galore!

Spencer Elkington 2026-01-15

Quartz, the web framework that this site is built on, is fantastic. The premise is simple — if you use something like Obsidian to write your notes in Markdown, all you’ve got to do is plug it in and it’ll spit out a website for you. It’s got enough built-in configuration that, if your goal is to minimize the time between “I want to publish a website” and “I have published a website”, it’s an awesome way to go.
