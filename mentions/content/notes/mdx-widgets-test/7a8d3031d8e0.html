Markdown has a lesser-known, still-delightful cousin called MDX. MDX’s goal is to have a foot on both sides of the fence: be primarily for writing, but have a bit of extra oomph under-the-hood to allow for website code to live alongside your writing. The problem? Just because something works with Markdown, doesn’t mean it’ll work with MDX.
