A Pattern Language, and a Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood

Spencer Elkington 2026-02-01

A few years ago, I stumbled on the 1977 urban planning and architecture reference manual A Pattern Language while researching more about design patterns for software. What I stumbled into, though, was a bit of hubris: software engineers didn’t invent design patterns! We could hardly be credited for coming up with the term, never mind the concept. Other fields had been using design patterns decades before the phrase ever got close to computing.

Unfortunately, I won’t be able to truly annotate the work in the same way I made the public annotations for College Admissions and the Stability of Marriage or The LLMs Starter. I lack, at the current time, a good way to automatically trim PDF or EPUB texts into preselected chapters. Additionally, the online source from Cornell is, unfortunately, missing quite a bit of the text (only 313 of the full 1169-page text —  a bit over 25%).
