In Review: The (bad) 2017 Robloxaville Remaster

Spencer Elkington 2024-02-25

When I was in high school, I dabbled a bit in programming on Roblox. Due to some pretty glaring security concerns, a project started over the summer of 2017 to remaster Robloxaville. I’ll be the first to admit that the remaster is substantially less fun to play than the original. While part of this constraint was time - the original was made over the course of ~4 years, while the remaster was made in ~3 months - it’s undeniable that the velocity of the original project was simply far higher because it was under-engineered.
