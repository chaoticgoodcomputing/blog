One of the pages under the `explorer` tag, which the cgc-tag-explorer specs open. It lists its
pages public first, then newest first, and pages of one day A→Z: Explorer Newest, Alpha, Bravo,
Oldest, then Locked, which is private (the fixture's second private tag, `secret`) though it is the
newest. Every title here starts "Explorer", so no other plugin's specs, which pick pages out by
title, meet one of these.
