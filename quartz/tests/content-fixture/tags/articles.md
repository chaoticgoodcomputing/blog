What the `articles` tag is for: longer pieces, written to be read from start to finish.

## How they are made

Each one grows out of notes, such as the [[nested/deep-note|deep note]].

A tag's description file, in the shape the vault's take at cutover (#43): `tags/<t>.md`, not
`tags/<t>/index.md`. The cgc-tag-page specs read it.
