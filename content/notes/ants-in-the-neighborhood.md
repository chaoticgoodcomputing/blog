# Ants in the Neighborhood

> Is Jane Street run by soccer-loving ants? Inconclusive. We *can* conclude that they're fans of Markov Chains, though — an invaluable tool for understanding complex data structures.

This post is a half-completed1 write-up of a [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/andys-morning-stroll-index/) problem about an ant traversing a soccer ball that I found particularly interesting. I found the answer awhile ago (it’s all Markov chains and isomorphism, *baybeeee!*), and thought that the analysis would actually be a fun application to the graphs formed by the [Pattern Languages](https://blog.chaoticgood.computer/content/notes/pattern-languages-of-the-neighborhood) project.

I may or may not get around to finishing this at some point, if the spark lights, or I end up wanting to take a crack at its [sequel problem](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/andys-afternoon-amble-index/). If you are interested, you should definitely [reach out!](https://blog.chaoticgood.computer/contact) Until then, though, appreciate some wonderful random walks and out-of-context diagrams.
