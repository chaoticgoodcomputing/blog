---
title: "#horticulture"
---

Horticulture is my catch-all term for the practice of [[public/content/notes/digital-gardening-with-quartz|digital gardening]]-- maintaining my knowledge, notes, and projects like tending a garden.

Most of the time, horticulture relates to maintaining this blog, along with other personal systems that either maintain my ability to manage my own life, and, in turn, might require their own maintenance over time.

I also use it, in general, when referring to [[public/content/notes/software-gardening.mdx|software gardening]]-- treating software development like agricultural work, that grows organically over time.
