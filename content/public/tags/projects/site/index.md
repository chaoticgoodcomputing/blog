`#projects/site` is a tag dedicated to the mechanical work done on this website. In general, this site is grown up in two places:

1. The [[tags/writing/index|writing]] and [[tags/horticulture/index|note-taking]] portions of this vault are managed with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), a fantastic notes app; and
2. The site — which takes the notes and publishes them — utilizes a version of the [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) framework with heavy [[tags/engineering/languages/typescript/index|TypeScript]] modifications for things like widgets and easier graph customizations.

My goal is to grow out the site itself to better fit the style and content I'm aiming for, in order to build it into [[tags/horticulture/seasons/systems/index|a better system]] for recording my thoughts and making the bearable ones publicly available. As I work more on Quartz, as well as mechanical customizations to Obsidian, any related notes will be published here.
