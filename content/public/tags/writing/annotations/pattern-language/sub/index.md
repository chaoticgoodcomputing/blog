These notes contain all pattern-specific annotations from [A Pattern Language](https://arl.human.cornell.edu/linked%20docs/Alexander_A_Pattern_Language.pdf) (by C. Alexander, S. Ishikawa, M. Silverstein et al.), a book setting up a design pattern and language for urban design and development.

These notes relate to **sub**structure patterns — those that deal with the internals of buildings, such as layouts and composition.
