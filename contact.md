Hi! My name is Spencer!

I’m a resident of Boston, Massachusetts, USA. I like to make cool things, especially when those things help other people make cool things, too!

During my [dayjob](https://blog.chaoticgood.computer/tags/projects/dayjob), I work as an AI Harness and Developer Experience Engineer at the Dallas and Washington D.C.-based [NWYC](https://nwyc.com/frontpage), helping folks connect with and understand their elected representatives.

The rest of the time, I’m typically:

-   Café-hopping, where I drink a copious amount of drip coffee and read1
-   Fiddling away at [projects](https://blog.chaoticgood.computer/tags/projects), either digital ([programming](https://blog.chaoticgood.computer/tags/engineering)) or physical (music and leatherwork)
-   Being gaslit by Stanley and Abby, my two terrible cats
